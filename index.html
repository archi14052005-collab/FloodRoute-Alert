BE PREPARED

Flood safety essentials

Simple precautions can help reduce risk during flooding.

01

Avoid floodwater

Never walk or drive through flooded roads. Water depth and road damage can be difficult to judge.

02

Follow official alerts

Check local authorities and disaster management advisories for verified emergency information.

03

Plan ahead

Keep emergency contacts, essential medicines and important documents accessible.
